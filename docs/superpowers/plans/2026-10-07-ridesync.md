# RideSync Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use `superpowers:executing-plans` to implement one issue at a time in this chat. Delegation is not requested by this plan. Steps use checkboxes for tracking.

**Goal:** Build a motorcycle remote for Insta360 X5, ONE RS, GO 3S and GoPro HERO12 Black, with independent GPS/motion logging to microSD.

**Architecture:** Camera profiles and vendor adapters share group intent and per-peer state/deadlines. A7670E GNSS, external IMU acquisition and motion estimation feed a bounded logger using a common monotonic timebase and UTC anchors; camera telemetry forwarding is an optional output.

**Tech stack:** Existing pinned PlatformIO/Arduino ESP32 scaffold, A7670E built-in GPS, microSD, external accelerometer/gyroscope; BLE stack and IMU selection are evidence-based deliverables before their integration.

**Spec:** [Architecture](../../architecture.md), [GoPro plan](../../gopro_plan.md), and [original brief](../../project_brief.md), supplemented by the user's HERO12 and GPS/motion logging requirements.

## Global constraints

- Target LILYGO TTGO T-A7670E R2 with A7670E built-in GPS.
- Support at least three cameras and an extensible registry; the complete target set is four models.
- No infinite waits; explicit timeouts, bounded queues/retries and independent camera failures.
- Requested recording, transport success and observed camera recording are distinct.
- Local GPS/motion logging continues independently of camera connectivity.
- Acceleration/lean/pitch need an additional IMU and validation; preserve raw measurements.
- GPS/IMU injection into HERO12 is outside the initial scope. No frame synchronization promise.
- Pin dependencies and audit licenses before copying code. Promote support only with evidence.
- Do not build every subsystem in one commit; each linked issue is a reviewable deliverable.

**GitHub tracker:** [https://github.com/llazzaro/RideSync/issues/16](https://github.com/llazzaro/RideSync/issues/16)

## Delivery phases

1. Evidence and contracts: tasks 1–2.
2. Single-camera control: X5 first (task 3), HERO12 independently (task 4).
3. Insta360 expansion and mixed control: tasks 5–7.
4. Controls and wake: tasks 8–9.
5. Independent GPS/SD logger: tasks 10–11; GNSS work can begin after task 1 without waiting for camera support.
6. Raw motion and derived estimates: tasks 12–13.
7. Optional Insta360 GPS output and integrated reliability: tasks 14–15.

## Review focus

- Ambiguous recording after lost responses/reset: tasks 3, 4, 7 and 15 prevent replay/toggle errors.
- One absent camera or incompatible BLE role: tasks 1, 2 and 7 verify bounded independent progress.
- Missing/slow/full SD and power loss: tasks 11 and 15 protect control timing and report data loss.
- Lost UTC, GNSS no-fix and rollover: tasks 10–11 preserve timestamp validity and monotonic ordering.
- Cornering forces, vibration and sensor drift: tasks 12–13 preserve raw data and flag unreliable estimates.

Task 2 adds a native PlatformIO test environment and CI coverage; its final configuration must document the exact native test command. Hardware tests are required for compatibility claims, not for honestly delivering an untested implementation.
