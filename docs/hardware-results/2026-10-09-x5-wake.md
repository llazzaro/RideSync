# X5 wake-only advertisement — October 9, 2026

After the separate pairing/shutter trial, the owner requested wake and confirmed
the X5 was off. The isolated diagnostic at `42a80bc` was built, independently
reviewed and uploaded, with all four written flash regions hash-verified. The
board's microSD slot remained empty. No camera recording was requested by this
experiment; wake mode refuses shutter commands.

The six-character identifier was derived privately from the previously
observed, owner-selected X5's full BLE name using the pinned MIT M5 example's
suffix rule. The BLE address was not substituted for that identifier. This
derivation is a source-based hypothesis for X5, not a verified universal rule.
Identifiers, raw transcripts and firmware-upload provenance remain outside Git.

## Experiment and observed transport result

One idle-only W configuration loaded the identifier into RAM, followed by one
A attempt. The raw legacy ADV contained exactly 31 bytes: flags plus a 26-byte
manufacturer value. The 25-byte scan response contained CE80 and the name
`Insta360 GPS Remote`. The full layout and pinned licensed sources are in the
[diagnostic README](../../tools/bench/x5_peripheral/README.md#finite-wake-only-experiment).
The requested SDK advertising duration was three seconds, without retry or a
subsequent normal-pairing advertisement.

Host synchronization occurred at boot time 1.318 s; the advertisement-complete
event arrived at 4.326 s. The policy stopped at 4.327 s with reason Deadline (3).
No peer connection, GATT subscription, read/write or shutter event was recorded.
All 16 events were reported without drops/truncation; final advertising and
SDK-in-flight states were zero. NVS refusal counters were init/open/erase `1/2/0`.

A concurrent passive Mac scan produced six callbacks matching the exact expected
manufacturer value. It also observed the expected remote name and CE80 service.
This independently confirms transmission of the intended source-derived
manufacturer value; scan callbacks do not constitute camera wake observations.
The camera-screen outcome is awaiting the owner's observation. No wake success
or incompatibility is claimed from the absent connection in this short window.

Post-trial readback of the 20 KiB NVS region at `0x9000` matched the preserved
factory baseline byte for byte. Readback reset the board into idle diagnostic
startup, with no further advertisement automatically started.

## Owner-requested repeat: observed wake

The owner explicitly requested another advertisement. Using the same installed
firmware, identifier, packet layout and three-second duration, a fresh idle
attempt synchronized at boot time 1.353 s and completed advertising at 4.367 s.
The owner then reported **“works!”**, confirming that the X5 woke on this
attempt. The earlier trial's camera-screen outcome remains unannotated; do not
retroactively classify it as either success or failure.

All 16 repeat events were reported without drops/truncation. No camera
connection, subscription, read/write or shutter event occurred. This establishes
an owner-observed wake on the current bench independently of recording or
reconnection. The camera firmware version, exact off-to-advertisement delay and
camera settings were not recorded; this is one observed success, not a firmware
compatibility matrix or wake reliability claim. Raw repeat evidence is retained
separately from the first trial.

The Mac again produced six scan callbacks matching the expected manufacturer
value. Post-repeat NVS readback was byte-identical to the first trial's verified
factory baseline; the board returned to idle diagnostic startup.

## Software checks and limits

Focused host policy/layout/input tests passed (root rerun 1.209 s); pinned probe
build passed (RAM 45,420 bytes, flash 573,965 bytes). The final-ELF audit passed
for raw wake-data preparation and addressed notify ownership, retained SDK
control, RAM-only store and NVS/core/PHY refusal guards. The current production
build also passed; it does not enable this isolated bench route.

The experiment does not implement production multi-camera wake scheduling,
connect/observe/start orchestration or camera recording-state semantics. #9,
#22 and the first-X5 recording milestone remain open. Reuse the observed
transmission result; another experiment needs a concrete unresolved question,
not an automatic repeat campaign.
