# Factory firmware bring-up — 2026-10-07

Observed locally through the board's USB serial bridge at 115200 baud. The ESP32
was running LILYGO's preinstalled AT-command firmware dated 2022-11-21, not
RideSync. No firmware was flashed, and no camera was connected/tested.

## Observed results

- Startup reports ESP32-D0WDQ5 revision 3 at 240 MHz.
- Factory diagnostic reports 4 MB flash and 4 MB usable PSRAM; this does not
  establish physical PSRAM capacity independently of the factory configuration.
- Modem identifies as A7670E-FASE; ATI reports A7670M7_V1.11.1 and CGMR reports
  A110B01A7670M7_F. Preserve both reported revision strings.
- Factory reports no SIM inserted; this did not prevent GNSS power-on.
- Opening serial repeatedly produced a fresh startup banner. Waiting for factory
  modem startup was required before commands received timely responses.
- The elapsed time from serial open/reset to first timely `AT` response was not
  measured. Use `AT` → `OK` as the command-ready gate; a numeric startup deadline
  remains unqualified and must be measured on a later bench run.
- GPS enable was accepted and GNSS later emitted READY. Both location queries
  returned empty fields: no position fix was observed during this bounded probe.

## Command excerpts

```text
AT
OK

AT+CGNSSPWR=1
OK

AT+CGNSSPWR?
+CGNSSPWR: 1
OK

AT+CGNSSINFO
+CGNSSINFO: ,,,,,,,,
OK
+CGNSSPWR: READY!

AT+CGPSINFO
+CGPSINFO: ,,,,,,,,
OK
```

GNSS-enabled, receiver-ready and valid-position-fix are separate states.
The probe confirms the first two, not the third. The initial location query
while GNSS was off returned ERROR; after enabling it returned empty/no-fix.

## Documentary pin and manual references

LILYGO's current [T-A7670X R2 guide](https://wiki.lilygo.cc/products/t-sim-series/t-a7670/)
links a drawing named **T-A7670X V1.4**. The physical PCB revision of this unit
was not recorded, so that drawing cannot yet be treated as its as-built
schematic. The vendor's pinned [ESP32 example definitions](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LILYGO/LilyGo-Modem-Series/blob/e8d8b82a23f324ef2ac1a24efe1c3b6cbabcca00/examples/S3_StandardSeries_External_GPS_Shield/utilities.h)
provide candidate UART, modem-control and SD pins in [hardware.md](../hardware.md);
they are not continuity-checked or approved for accessory wiring. Several
candidate pins are ESP32 boot straps, and SD overlaps strap pins.

The SIMCom-authored [A76XX AT Command Manual V1.09](https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/A7670E-Cat-1-GNSS-HAT/A76XX_Series_AT_Command_Manual_V1.09.pdf),
hosted by Waveshare, is the dated reference used to interpret the observed GNSS commands. SIMCom's
current [document index](https://cn.simcom.com/technical_files-p2.html?filetype=0&pro_cat=0&pro_li=83&time=0)
lists V2.03; neither listing establishes which revision is normative for the
observed module firmware. Preserve this uncertainty until a matching module
manual/firmware qualification is available.
