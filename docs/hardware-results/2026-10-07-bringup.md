# Factory firmware bring-up — 2026-10-07

Observed locally through the board's USB serial bridge at 115200 baud. The ESP32
was running LILYGO's preinstalled AT-command firmware dated 2022-11-21, not
RideSync. No firmware was flashed, and no camera was connected/tested.

## Observed results

- Startup reports ESP32-D0WDQ5 revision 3 at 240 MHz.
- Factory diagnostic reports 4 MB flash and 4 MB usable PSRAM; this does not
  establish physical PSRAM capacity independently of the factory configuration.
- Modem identifies as A7670E-FASE; ATI reports A7670M7_V1.11.1 and CGMR reports
  A110B01A7670M7_F. Preserve both reported revision strings.
- Factory reports no SIM inserted; this did not prevent GNSS power-on.
- Opening serial repeatedly produced a fresh startup banner. Waiting for factory
  modem startup was required before commands received timely responses.
- GPS enable was accepted and GNSS later emitted READY. Both location queries
  returned empty fields: no position fix was observed during this bounded probe.

GNSS-enabled, receiver-ready and valid-position-fix are separate states.
The probe confirms the first two, not the third. The initial location query
while GNSS was off returned ERROR; after enabling it returned empty/no-fix.
